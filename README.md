# Sales & Employees Analysis Dashboards

This project presents **interactive dashboards** designed to analyze **sales performance and employee data**, helping businesses monitor performance and make faster, data-driven decisions based on clear and structured insights.

The project was built using **Microsoft Excel**, applying **Data Modeling with Power Pivot** to create relationships between tables and ensure accurate calculations and professional metrics.

---

## Sales Dashboard

An interactive dashboard designed to analyze **sales performance**, including:

- Total Sales, Total Quantity, and Number of Customers
- Sales analysis by **Country** to identify top-performing markets
- **Top Customers** and their impact on revenue
- **Top Selling Products**
- Order status distribution (**Delivered / Undelivered**)
- Most used **Payment Methods**
- Performance tracking across **years**
- Interactive filters by **Date, Country, and Order Status**

### Business Value
Helps management identify the most profitable markets, improve delivery operations, and support marketing and inventory decisions based on reliable data.

---

## Employees Analysis Dashboard

A dashboard for analyzing **employee performance and HR insights**, including:

- Total number of employees
- Average salary
- Average employee ratings
- Employee distribution by **Employment Type**  
  (Full-Time / Contract / Part-Time / Hourly)
- Average salary analysis by **Department**
- **Top-performing employees** based on ratings
- Sales team productivity analysis  
  (**Sales Quantity by Employee**)
- Interactive filters by **Department** and **Employment Type**

### Business Value
Supports management in evaluating employee performance, improving HR decision-making, and optimizing incentives and training strategies.
